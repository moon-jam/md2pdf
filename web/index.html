md2pdf

The preview on the right is the actual PDF layout — real pagination, real margins — not just a styled HTML page. Edit on the left, see your PDF on the right.

  • Open Folder to mount a local directory. Relative image paths like ![](img/photo.png) resolve automatically, nothing leaves your machine.
  • Paste or drag images directly into the editor. Right-click images in the File Explorer to insert at the cursor. Resize with ![](img.png){width=300} or {width=50% height=auto}.
  • Add <!-- pagebreak --> anywhere to force a page break — watch it appear in the preview immediately.
  • Write LaTeX math: inline $E = mc^2$ and display $$...$$ blocks render with KaTeX.
  • Open the Custom CSS panel to adjust fonts, colours, spacing. Live preview updates as you type.
  • Export .zip to download the Markdown file together with its images, ready to re-open or share.
  • The title field at the top becomes the PDF filename when you save.
  • Everything saves automatically in this browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
